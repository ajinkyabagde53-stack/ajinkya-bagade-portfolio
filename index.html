About
Four years.
Many hats.
No half measures.

I started in content, learning how words shape perception, how a single line can make someone stop scrolling. That foundation never left me. It just got bigger. Over four years I moved from writing to strategy, from strategy to creative direction, from creative direction to leading a team that does all of it.

At Novel Office, I built the brand's voice, tone and guidelines from the ground up, and then made sure they showed up consistently across every channel, every campaign and every piece of collateral we touched. I don't just run campaigns. I build the systems behind them.

AI is now central to how I work, not as a shortcut, but as a force multiplier. I use it every day across content, design and code. I’m currently building AI agents from scratch and actively upskilling in how they’re architected and deployed. The intersection of marketing and machine intelligence is where I spend a lot of my thinking time.

Building a Lifecycle Marketing System
Designed and implemented automated email nurture workflows, onboarding, re-engagement and retention flows with proper segmentation and A/B testing.

Built a structured lead management system that handles every enquiry, from the moment someone submits a website form or writes directly via email, through to full team notification and follow-up. Every step is defined, every person knows their role, and no lead falls through.

On contact, a thank-you email is automatically triggered to the lead. Internally, the enquiry flows through a defined pipeline: admin filter → ERP entry → marketing handoff → engagement summary → full team briefing.

Novel Office · Meta Ads
Performance Ad Creatives

Oversaw the paid social strategy for Novel Office across Meta. Campaigns structured across three funnel objectives, building awareness with cold audiences, re-engaging warm traffic, and converting intent with direct offers. AI tools were used throughout, from brief generation and copy iteration to audience messaging refinement, compressing production time without losing creative quality.

Brand Awareness
Top-of-funnel creatives served to cold audiences. Goal: introduce Novel Office as a premium managed workspace and drive initial brand recall.
Retargeting
Mid-funnel creatives served to warm audiences, people who'd visited the site, watched a video, or interacted with previous ads. Goal: bring them back and reinforce the proposition.
Lead Generation
Bottom-of-funnel creative with a direct incentive. Goal: convert high-intent prospects into enquiries and trial bookings with a time-sensitive offer.
