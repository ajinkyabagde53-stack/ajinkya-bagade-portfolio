Rules of engagement

So a slow day doesn't trigger a bad decision.
Hold — it's noise

A campaign that has spent less than its own CPL hasn't earned a verdict. Zero leads on small spend is expected. Don't touch it.

Investigate — it's signal

Zero leads after 2–3× the target CPL in spend is real waste. Check placements and search terms, then fix or pause.

Diagnose before you cut

Low CTR is a creative / targeting problem; healthy CTR but low conversion rate is a landing-page problem. The funnel tells you which.

Judge on value, not cost

A ₹2,400 lead that closes beats a ₹500 lead that never converts. Fill in deals — the target may be the number that's wrong.
